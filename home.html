Overview

Project Details

Choosing a career path can feel overwhelming. Many existing resources offer generic advice that fails to consider individual circumstances, leaving users without clear direction. Forge addresses this gap by providing a personalized mentorship experience through a friendly, chat-based interface that feels like talking to a knowledgeable advisor.


The application needed to feel approachable rather than intimidating, welcoming users who may already feel uncertain about their futures. The design emphasizes simplicity and warmth, using conversational language and a clean layout that puts users at ease. Close collaboration with design students ensured the interface remained intuitive, allowing anyone—regardless of technical comfort—to receive meaningful career guidance through natural conversation.
